Named for a road that goes somewhere.

The Lincoln Highway was the first road built to cross the United States. It still runs through Dalton.

Before the interstates, there was one road stitched together out of farm lanes and main streets so that a person could drive from New York to San Francisco. It came through Ohio, and it came through Dalton. It is very likely the road you are on right now.

We took the name because a road is a plain thing with a plain purpose. It is not a destination. It exists to get you somewhere, and every mile of it is a decision to keep going in one direction.

Lincoln Highway Baptist Church is a new work, sent out from First Baptist Church of Jackson in Massillon, where our people were taught, baptised and sent. We are independent and Baptist by conviction, and we preach from the King James Bible without ornament.

We are small, and we are new. If you come, you will not disappear into a crowd. That is either exactly what you are hoping for or exactly what you are afraid of. Either way, come.

What we believe, stated plainly.

We would rather you know exactly what you are walking into. Nothing here waits until your third visit.

  • The ScripturesThe Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God. Sixty-six books, the only complete and final authority for faith and practice. We preach and read from the King James Version.
  • GodOne true God, eternally existing in three persons, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, equal in every divine perfection.
  • Jesus ChristConceived of the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He lived without sin, died in the place of sinners, rose bodily the third day, and ascended.
  • SalvationA gift of God, by grace, through personal faith in Christ alone. The new birth is instantaneous rather than a process, and the believer is eternally secure.
  • The ChurchA living body with Christ as its Head. The local church is a congregation of baptised believers joined by covenant to worship, to disciple, and to go.
  • The OrdinancesBaptism by immersion, once, after faith. The Lord's Supper, kept as a memorial by born-again members of the church.
  • Things to ComeChrist will take the church to Himself before the Tribulation, then return personally, bodily and visibly to establish His kingdom.
  • SeparationBelievers from worldly practice, the church from false teaching, and the church from the state.

Everything you would otherwise have to guess at.

What to wear

Come in what you have. You will see suits and you will see jeans, and nobody is keeping count. We would far rather have you here in what you own than not here at all.

How long it lasts

Sunday School runs about an hour, then a short break before worship at 10:15. The morning service is usually finished a little after 11:30. Singing, prayer, and preaching straight out of the text.

Bringing children

Please do. There are classes for every age during Sunday School, and children are welcome in the service. Nobody here is bothered by the noise a child makes.

Will I be singled out?

No. We will be glad you came and we will say so, but you will not be asked to stand, to give, or to fill anything in. Sit in the back if you would rather.
