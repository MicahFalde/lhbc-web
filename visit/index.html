What to expect on a Sunday morning

  • Where to go

    Kingsway Christian School, on the old Lincoln Highway between Dalton and Orrville. Park in the school lot and come in through the main doors. Someone will be there to point the way.

  • What it is like

    One service at 10:15 a.m. Worship and preaching straight from the Bible.

  • Bringing children

    Please do. There is a nursery for babies through pre-K and classes for Kindergarten through Fifth grade, with a check-in table at the door. Your children are also welcome to sit with you in the service.

  • What to wear

    There is no dress code. While our pastor will be in a suit and tie and some of our people dress up for the Lord's day, others come as they are. You are welcome either way.

  • What will be asked of me?

    Only the short guest card in your chair, so we know you came and can thank you. You will not be asked to stand, to speak, or to give.
